## 🏛️ Phase 1: THE INCEPTION (Nov 2025)
**Status**: 🟢 COMPLETE
**Commit Pattern**: `init: sovereign kernel`

- **Core Identity**: Deployment of the `IdentityManager` and RBAC foundations.
- **Genetic Blueprint**: First definition of the shared Types and Barrels.
- **Objective**: Establishing a secure, multi-tenant capable entrance to the system.

## 📦 Phase 2: THE FLOW ENGINE (Dec 2025)
**Status**: 🟢 COMPLETE
**Commit Pattern**: `feat: stock engine & deterministic flows`

- **Inventory Dominance**: Creation of the `StockEngine` with real-time deduction logic.
- **Atomic State**: Transition from standard `useState` to **Jotai Atoms** for better performance.
- **Objective**: Mapping the physical movement of goods into digital, immutable events.

## 🚀 Phase 3: FLEET DOMINATION (Jan-Feb 2026)
**Status**: 🟢 COMPLETE
**Commit Pattern**: `feat: fleet commander & multi-instance bridge`

- **Scalability**: Deployment of the `FleetCommander` to manage thousands of independent restaurant nodes.
- **Isolation**: Hardening the `SovereignGuard` to prevent any data leakage between tenants.
- **Objective**: Building the infrastructure for a global SaaS empire.

## ⚖️ Phase 4: FISCAL LAW (March 2026)
**Status**: 🟢 COMPLETE
**Commit Pattern**: `fix: nf525 compliance & sha-256 chain`

- **Legal Armor**: Integration of the `FiscalEngine`. Inalterability guaranteed by SHA-256 chain-linking.
- **Integrity**: Every transaction is sealed and indexed for tax transparency.
- **Objective**: Ensuring the system is legally inattaquable in high-regulation markets (France/EU).
