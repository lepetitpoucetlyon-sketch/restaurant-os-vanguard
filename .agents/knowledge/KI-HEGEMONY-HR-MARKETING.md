# KI: Hegemony Strategy (HR & Marketing)

## 🎯 HR Hegemony (Brigade Management)
- **Mission**: Orchestrate the humans behind the machine. Recruitment, payroll, and planning.
- **Defense**: PII (Personally Identifiable Information) encryption is mandatory. No staff personal data should leaked to any other hegemonies except for scheduling nodes in OPS.

## 🎯 Marketing Hegemony (Empire Growth)
- **Mission**: Expansion and intelligence. CRM, SEO, and AI-driven reservations.
- **Defense**: Connectors to external social/marketing APIs must be isolated within this sector to prevent third-party scripts from crawling the Backoffice.
