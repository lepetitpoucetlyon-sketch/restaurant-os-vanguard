# KI: Hegemony Strategy (OPS / Operational)

## 🎯 Identity & Mission
The **OPS Hegemony** is the "Live Engine" of the restaurant. Its mission is to handle real-time production: Kitchen (KDS), Bar, Floor Plan, and Point of Sale (POS).

## 🛡️ Defense Strategy
- **Zero-Latency**: Logic must be optimized for real-time order routing.
- **Resilience**: Off-line first capabilities (via Nexus Sync) to survive network failures.
- **Tactile Precision**: UI is optimized for touch-screens and fast-paced environments.

## 🧩 Sub-Sectors
- `kds`: Kitchen Display System (Order tracking).
- `bar`: Bar management and beverage dispensing.
- `floor-plan`: Real-time table status and reservation mapping.
- `pos`: Unified Point of Sale interface.
